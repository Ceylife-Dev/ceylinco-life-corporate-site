# Ceylinco Life website: working rules

@AGENTS.md

This file is read at the start of every session. Follow it exactly. The full
design-system reference is `docs/design-system.md`; the tokens are in
`src/styles/tokens.css`.

## About the company

Ceylinco Life is an **insurance and financial protection company**: life,
retirement, medical, investment and group plans, and possibly more. Never
describe it as only a life insurance company, in copy, comments or docs. Use
"insurance", "plans" or "protection" for general statements, and name "life
insurance" only when you mean that specific product.

## Working agreement (how to work with the designer)

- The person you work with is a UI/UX designer. Explain things in plain language.
- **Ask, don't guess.** If anything affecting design, behaviour, links, copy or
  structure is unclear or could be decided more than one way, ask. Give your
  recommended default with each question so the answer can be "yes".
- Group questions and ask them together, before starting a piece of work.
  Don't ask about trivial details you can decide safely: list those as assumptions.
- If an instruction conflicts with something in the project, say so and ask which wins.
- Work in phases and stop for review at the end of each phase.
- Report honestly: say what was tested and what was not.

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS v4 (as a PostCSS plugin),
Lucide icons, `next/font`. Next.js 16 differs from older versions: read the
relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before using an API.
`cacheComponents` is on: a Client Component that reads the URL
(`usePathname`) must be inside `<Suspense>` on routes with dynamic params.

## HARD RULES (apply to everything)

1. **Never pure black** (`#000`, `black`) for text, borders or shadows. Text is the warm near-black `ink` (#2A2A2E).
2. **Radius:** buttons 12px, inputs and selects 12px, cards and panels 24px. Anything nested inside a card or panel is 12px so curves are concentric. Utilities: `rounded-control` (12), `rounded-container` (24). Circles (`rounded-full`) are allowed **only** for the Stepper's number badges, the round icon on the advisor card, the FAQ plus / minus toggle, the 56px help circle above the FAQ heading, the pink phone circle in the FAQ strip, the circular arrow on the product cards, the large soft decorative circle behind the app phone, the footer social icon buttons, and the hero's previous / next buttons; nothing else is a circle.
3. **Borders and shadows are grey, never black.** Shadows are grey-tinted `rgb(71 76 85 / 0.10)`: `shadow-sm`, `shadow-md`, `shadow-lg` (mega menu panel uses `shadow-lg`).
4. **Friendly feel:** warm, generous spacing, soft 150-200ms transitions, no sharp or heavy decorative shapes. Brand maroon is used **sparingly and deliberately**: primary button, active nav item, icons, number badges, link underlines and key numbers, not large fills. **Soft pink (`brand-tint` #FBEEF0, `brand-tint-strong` #F4D9DD) is part of the look again** (restored at the designer's request): icon and step tiles, the advisor card, chips, hover and pressed backgrounds, the circular card arrow on hover and the accordion toggle. It is never a large full-bleed fill. Section backgrounds are `<Section tone>`: `white`, `alt` (`surface-section` #FBFAF9, a warm off-white), `tint` (pink; available but not used on the home page now) and `dark`; no two neighbours share a tone. Home page order and tones (the order of the Figma frame): Hero (photo) | products (white) | getting covered (alt) | trust (dark) | testimonials (white) | app (alt) | news (white) | final CTA (alt, with the maroon card) | footer (dark). The FAQ is not on the home page (removed at the designer's request; its component and wording are kept, unused). The Figma frame shows testimonials on alt, but beside the alt app section that would break the alternation, so testimonials are white. The warm cream `surface-cream` (#FFFBF2) fills the slim quick-link row, the advisor card and the FAQ contact strip. There is no Claims section on the home page (claims links stay in the menu, footer, FAQ and step 4 of Getting covered). The dark `footer-bg` (#1F1D21) is used only for the trust block and the footer; the closing call-to-action is a rounded 24px maroon card on an alt section and is the only maroon block. Text links and icons on dark are white, never maroon. Cards on a white or alt tone: white, 1px `border`, `shadow-md` (`shadow-sm` for the product, testimonial, news and FAQ cards, as in the Figma frame); on pink, things inside are white.
5. **Accessibility:** all text/background pairs meet WCAG AA (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large text and UI components). Visible focus ring on every interactive element. Touch targets at least 44x44px (`min-h-touch`, `min-w-touch`). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (use the token durations, which drop to ~0).
6. **Typeface:** Plus Jakarta Sans, weights 400/500/600/700 only, via `next/font` with `display: swap`. Fallback stack: "Plus Jakarta Sans", "Noto Sans Sinhala", "Noto Sans Tamil", system-ui, sans-serif. Body 16-18px, line-height 1.5-1.6. Headings SemiBold or Bold, line-height 1.2-1.3, slight negative letter spacing (-1% to -2%) on large headings only. Sinhala and Tamil get slightly larger size and line-height (handled by `:root:lang(si|ta)` in tokens.css; no negative tracking for them).
7. **Everything comes from tokens.** No hard-coded colours, radii, shadows, font sizes, z-indexes or arbitrary Tailwind values (`p-[13px]`, `bg-[#fff]`, `z-50`). Need something new? Add a token to `src/styles/tokens.css` first (and tell the designer), then use it.

## Text case review toggle

A review tool (development, or `?review=1`; never in production for visitors or in standalone mode) switches UI text between sentence case and title case; see `docs/design-system.md`. Rules to follow: write all content in sentence case (the content files are the one source of truth); title case comes from `toTitleCase()` in `src/lib/case.ts`, never from `text-transform: capitalize`; new UI text that should follow the toggle (headings, card titles, nav and menu labels, buttons, text links, footer links, bottom bar labels) gets `{...caseProps}`; body copy, quotes, names, dates and phone numbers do not; add new acronyms and proper nouns to `CASE_EXCEPTIONS`.

## Installable app (PWA): the project is a PWA from now on

Full detail is in `docs/design-system.md` section 12. The rules to follow in every task:

- **Do not change the desktop design** for PWA work. App-style changes apply to phones and tablets or to standalone mode only.
- **Manifest** (`src/app/manifest.ts`): theme colour = `brand`, background = `surface`, read through `colourToken()` (`src/lib/tokens.ts`), never typed as hex. Icons come from `scripts/make-pwa-icons.mjs` (192, 512, maskable 512, Apple touch icon).
- **Service worker** (`public/sw.js`, production only): **never cache or intercept the customer portal, payments, accounts, sign-in, forms, search, APIs, query-string pages or anything private/authenticated.** When you add a route like that, add its start to `NEVER_CACHE` and bump `VERSION`. Offline copy for public pages only; the offline page is `/offline`.
- **Bottom bar** (`BottomNav`) shows at **768px and below in any display mode** (browser tab or installed app; it must never depend on `display-mode: standalone`): Home, Products, Pay, Quote, More (one-line 12px labels in five equal columns; Pay is read out as "Pay premium online" and Quote as "Get a quote"; Quote goes to the quote page; Claims is not a tab, it stays in the menu under Claims & Support). Hidden from 769px. 64px plus the safe area, a 1px top border and a soft upward shadow (`--shadow-up`), `z-dropdown` (ABOVE the full-screen menu, so "More" stays visible), with body bottom padding so it never covers content. "More" (three dots) is the ONLY menu entry point on phones: `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls="mobile-menu"`, the same maroon active look (icon, label, 2px top bar) while the menu is open (the current page goes grey meanwhile), and focus returns to it when the menu closes. **Phone header** at 768px and below: a compact 56px bar (`--size-header-mobile`, plus the top safe area in the installed app): logo left (32px high, the existing logo; no compact asset without the tagline exists yet), search and language right (44px tap targets); NO menu button (it only exists from 769px to the nav breakpoint). The phone menu has its own close button, closes on Esc, traps focus and locks page scroll.
- **"Add to home screen" card** (`InstallPrompt`): repeat visits only, dismissible, quiet for 30 days, uses `beforeinstallprompt` (no card where the browser lacks it).
- **Accessibility patterns (WCAG 2.2 AA; details in `docs/design-system.md` section 14).** The page behind an open overlay (phone menu, search dialog) is `inert` through `useFocusTrap`; the bottom bar has `data-inert-exempt`. A menu that must take focus when it opens is shown at once (fade the opacity only, never the visibility). Landmarks never share a name; links that open a new tab say so; a link’s name contains its visible text. New icon-only controls need a label; new pages need `<main id="main-content">` and one h1.
- **No hover-only interactions.** Every `hover:` style needs `active:` / `group-active:` and `focus-visible:` equivalents. Touch targets stay 44px or more.
- **Fixed elements respect safe areas** (`pt-safe`, `pb-safe`, `fixed-corner`, `fixed-install` in `globals.css`). At 768px and below the scroll reveal rises 12px (token); for `prefers-reduced-motion` there is no reveal and numbers do not count.
- All PWA wording lives in `src/content/pwa.ts`. Loading states use `Skeleton` (`@/components/ui`).
